# Accuracy and scope

This release packages the existing **Revision 25** campus model. It is a visual reconstruction based on public photographs, mapped footprints, floor-plan references and elevation data. It is not an as-built architectural model, a measured survey, or a record that every campus audit item has been completed.

## Coverage

The scene extends from the Winthrop precinct in the north to the southern campus and Business School. Reid Library, EZONE and EECE, Winthrop and BJM contain more developed architectural and interior geometry. Many other buildings have photograph-guided exterior detail, estimated floors and glazing, with incomplete room layouts. Hidden archival collections preserve some earlier geometry for reference and are excluded from normal rendering.

Revision 25 merges the completed Music entrance, Hackett forecourt and Irwin patches into Revision 24. It retains earlier work on the Sunken Garden, Bayliss, Tattersall, Shenton, Geology, Mathematics, University Club, Somerville, New Fortune, Monadelphous, approaches, stairs and local ground transitions. Coverage and confidence vary by place.

## Significant estimates

- Building heights, concealed elevations, roof service equipment, planting positions and some room arrangements are estimates.
- The coordinate axes are east X, north Y and up Z, in metres. Footprints are aligned to a local campus frame. A nominal metric unit does not make every dimension measured.
- BJM's original linked model was fitted with XY scale approximately 1.135 and unchanged vertical scale. Its footprint fitting error is about 2.05 m RMS.
- Terrain comes from 5 m elevation data, with finer interpolated meshes and local modelling adjustments. Steps, thresholds, ramp ends, retaining walls and drains are often photo-derived. They are not suitable for construction or accessibility decisions.
- Socrates uses an existing scan with an estimated lower plinth. Diotima is a single-photo reconstruction with uncertain back and side surfaces. Its Greek side inscriptions are not reconstructed.
- Ceres and the Geology panel use photographs with inferred shallow depth. Lighting baked into source photographs and low source resolution limit their appearance.
- The Reid bridge mural and ENCM artwork are simplified interpretations. Occluded sections are inferred.
- Music's unobserved service rooms and complete roof, parts of Hackett's surrounding context, and the exact placement of some Irwin finishes remain incomplete or approximate.
- Vegetation assets approximate the landscape; species and individual tree forms are not surveyed.

## What the checks establish

The historical R25 report records successful reopening and scoped checks for the three merged patches and prior R24 checks. Those checks cover selected geometry, protection of surrounding objects, contacts and transitions. They do not prove the whole campus matches reality or that all hidden intersections are absent.

The publication check separately verifies that the released file opens, the three libraries and file textures remain packed, required collections exist, an overview camera is available, and no external fonts are needed. Gallery images are renders of this model. Publication preparation does not add new architectural features.
